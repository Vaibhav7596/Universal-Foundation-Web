CSSI Program

The 21-Day Internship Experience

A hands-on training program combining academic learning with real-world social engagement — designed to transform students into socially responsible leaders.

What You Will Experience

  • Cybersecurity awareness training
  • Tree plantation drives
  • Fire safety and emergency drills
  • Flood rescue demonstrations
  • Cleanliness and Swachh Bharat campaigns
  • NGO operations and planning sessions

Key Learning Outcomes

  • Practical problem-solving skills
  • Teamwork and leadership under pressure
  • Real-world exposure to social work
  • Disaster management understanding
  • Strong sense of social responsibility
Training Approach

How Each Day Is Structured

Each day of the 21-day program focuses on a specific theme — from disaster management to digital safety. Participants engage in field visits, group training sessions, and collaborative projects guided by experienced mentors from the field.

Why This Matters

Bridging Theory & Reality

Most academic programs stop at theory. This internship bridges the gap by placing students in real environments — flood sites, community centres, schools — so they develop the instincts, empathy, and skills that textbooks can't teach.
