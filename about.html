Who We Are

A Legacy of Community Service

Universal Foundation is a non-governmental organization focused on civil defense, disaster management, and community empowerment. Since its establishment in 2000, the organization has worked tirelessly to train individuals and spread awareness about safety and responsibility across Gujarat and beyond.

Our Founder

Prakashkumar Vekariya

The foundation was established by Prakashkumar Vekariya, a respected social leader and recipient of the President's Award for his distinguished service. His unwavering vision and dedication have shaped the organization into a trusted institution that communities across Gujarat rely on.

Our Mission

To empower individuals with practical knowledge and life-saving skills, enabling them to respond effectively to emergencies and contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Our Vision

To build a resilient society where every individual is aware, prepared, and capable of handling challenges responsibly — creating a safer India for future generations.

Our Programs

What We Do

Flood Rescue & Relief

Rapid-response operations providing aid and rescue during flood emergencies across Gujarat.

Fire Safety Training

Hands-on emergency drills and fire safety workshops for schools, workplaces, and communities.

Cyber Security Awareness

Educating citizens about digital safety to protect against online fraud and threats.

Tree Plantation Drives

Large-scale planting initiatives to improve green cover and environmental sustainability.

Swachh Bharat Campaigns

Active participation in Swachh Bharat and Nirmal Gujarat initiatives for cleaner communities.

Women's Empowerment

Safety awareness and empowerment programs designed to uplift women in the community.
